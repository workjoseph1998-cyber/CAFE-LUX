# Visual assets

The design follows the user-provided reference: espresso black, dark chocolate, metallic gold, champagne highlights, serif headlines and warm amber coffee imagery. The original chat screenshots/packaging were visible as design references but were not present as downloadable image files in the execution workspace. No original packaging asset is falsely claimed as bundled. Upload the originals using the admin media library to replace the defaults.

`cafe/static/images/hero.jpg`, `drink.jpg` and `ground.jpg` are AI-generated editorial coffee illustrations optimized as JPEG. It is decorative brand imagery, not a photograph of an actual Café LUX location or a claim about a product. Product fallback images are generated cup/pouch illustrations, labeled as illustrative on the product page; upload actual product photos before selling. No photo taken from an inaccessible third-party URL is bundled.

The hero includes a real animated SVG pouring path and steam. Coffee-bean rotation/drop and grounds particles animate in the ground-coffee section; the lifestyle cup lifts/tilts in a sipping gesture with steam. These are lightweight stylized animations, not video of a real person drinking coffee. Section reveals and product hover transitions are CSS/JS. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; that preference is used for stable screenshots.

User-uploaded product, logo, lifestyle and café photography can replace these defaults without code edits. Uploads should be owned or licensed by the operator. No fabricated real prices, address, telephone number, sourcing certification or awards appear in the shipped catalog. The screenshot runner uses conspicuously labeled synthetic fixtures in an ephemeral database.

Version 1.3 adds generated espresso.jpg, iced.jpg, turkish.jpg and chocolate.jpg. They are fallback illustrations to distinguish drink families, not actual product/location photographs. Uploaded catalog photography always takes priority. No third-party photo URL is required.
