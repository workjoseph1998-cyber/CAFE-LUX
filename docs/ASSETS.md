# Visual assets

Version 2 uses ivory #F8F4ED, warm sand, coffee #8D5A3B, espresso #4B3025 and restrained gold. Typography uses local system sans-serif and occasional serif headlines; no third-party scripts, fonts or tracking load.

The existing repository's `hero.jpg`, `drink.jpg` and `ground.jpg` are retained AI-generated editorial illustrations. They are illustrative, not photographs of an actual Café LUX location or actual product packaging. The uploaded chat references informed the redesign, but original full-resolution packaging photography was not available as a downloadable workspace file. Upload the original packaging/photo through Product images and select it on the product; never treat fallback artwork as the actual package.

No new third-party stock photos or unverified licensed imagery is bundled. Uploaded photos should be owned/licensed by the business. The screenshot runner installs only temporary synthetic offers and labels them as such; they are not seeded by setup.

Motion consists of gentle steam, card reveals, hover zoom and smooth transitions. Reduced-motion disables decorative motion. No heavyweight video or external animation library is used.
