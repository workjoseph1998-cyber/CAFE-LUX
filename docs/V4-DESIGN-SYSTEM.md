# V4 — Quiet Ritual

Porcelain `#fafbf7`, forest `#173d32`, botanical `#e8eee1`, white surfaces and muted green text replace Espresso Editorial. The new homepage has a split typographic hero and arched photograph, a quiet value strip, two collection portals, independent editable sections, real delivered-order bestsellers and a Finder invitation. No invented customer ratings or sales claims.

System sans typography, generous whitespace, pill calls to action, rounded product cards, consistent forms and a separate forest-sidebar workspace. All assets load locally; owner-selected photographs take precedence over bundled illustration. Favorites are separate form controls, avoiding nested links/buttons. Quick add appears only for one available variant with no extras or option choice; all other cards lead to customization. The existing product price/gallery controls, sticky mobile product action and server-backed bag remain progressive enhancements.

Responsive navigation collapses before tablet controls collide. Customer pages support 320px and up, Arabic RTL, keyboard focus, accessible native forms/dialogs and reduced motion. The public account/recovery/address screens and admin views share the same design language. Current synthetic screenshots are under `screenshots/v4`; no sample content is injected into customer installations.
